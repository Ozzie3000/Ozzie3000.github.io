How to Play
  • Move: your highlighted player (green ring) follows the mouse — or your finger on touch screens.
  • Kick: click (or quick-tap) to fire the ball toward that spot — you must be next to the ball.
  • Switch player: spacebar cycles your three players; on touch, tap the player you want. Auto-switch (nearest to ball) resumes a few seconds later.
  • Dribble: just run into the ball to push it along.
  • Kickoff: both teams hold their own half until the kicking team touches the ball.
  • Match: two halves (length slider), 1-minute halftime, teams swap ends.
  • Difficulty: 1–10 slider changes AI speed and shot accuracy.
